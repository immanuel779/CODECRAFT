About Codecraft

Empowering the next generation of Nigerian frontend developers

Our Story

Founded in 2025 with a simple belief: quality tech education should be accessible to everyone.

What started as small WhatsApp-based HTML & CSS classes has grown into a structured learning community with lifetime-access courses, real projects, daily support, and hundreds of success stories from students now working as frontend developers across Nigeria and beyond.

Codecraft was born from a simple belief: quality tech education should be accessible to everyone, not just those who can afford expensive bootcamps or travel abroad.

Meet Our Founder

The passionate educator behind Codecraft

Oluwadamilare Opeyemi

Founder & Lead Instructor

"I believe that anyone with passion and dedication can become a great developer. My goal is to make that journey as smooth and practical as possible."

With hands-on experience in modern frontend development and a passion for teaching, Opeyemi has helped hundreds of beginners go from zero to landing their first developer roles. His teaching style is direct, patient, and focused on what actually matters in the 2026 job market.

Before founding Codecraft, Opeyemi worked as a frontend developer at several tech companies, building real-world applications that serve thousands of users. He brings that practical experience directly into the classroom.
