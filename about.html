About CodeCraft

CodeCraft is a premium online learning platform dedicated to helping Nigerians and Africans build real-world frontend development skills — fast, practically, and affordably.

Our Story

Founded in 2025 by Oluwadamilare Opeyemi in Lagos, Nigeria, CodeCraft was born from a simple belief: quality tech education should be accessible to everyone, not just those who can afford expensive bootcamps or travel abroad.

What started as small WhatsApp-based HTML & CSS classes has grown into a structured learning community with lifetime-access courses, real projects, daily support, and hundreds of success stories from students now working as frontend developers across Nigeria and beyond.

Our Core Values
